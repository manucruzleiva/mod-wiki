# Cobblemon Trainer's Satchel

A trainer's bag, not a backpack. The Satchel sits on your back and gives every kind of item its own
pocket: Poké Balls, medicine, evolution stones, seeds, building blocks, drops and key items, so the
Ultra Ball is always where you left it.

!!! warning "Not released yet"
    The Trainer's Satchel has no public download yet. These pages describe the current development
    build, **0.0.14**: seven pockets with tag-based sorting and server rules, three tiers, sorting and slot
    locks, keep-on-death, a battle lock with optional limits, evolution hints, planting from the bag, a
    PokeBelt refill, the bag drawn on your back, and an unlimited item storage behind every Cobblemon PC.

## Highlights

- 🎒 **[Seven pockets](pockets.md#the-seven-pockets)**: each takes only its own kind of item, and
  anything else bounces back.
- 🏷️ **[Sorted by tags](pockets.md#which-pocket-an-item-goes-to)**: items from any mod land in the right
  pocket, and operators can send any item to the pocket they choose, in game.
- ⬆️ **[Three tiers](tiers.md)**: Canvas Satchel, Leather Explorer Bag and Ultra Satchel, from 14 to 42
  slots per pocket, upgraded without losing what is inside.
- 🔢 **[Sorting and slot locks](tiers.md#sorting-and-slot-locks)**: from the Explorer Bag up.
- ⚔️ **[Battle rules](in-the-field.md#in-a-battle)**: during a Cobblemon battle only the battle pockets
  open, with optional per-battle limits.
- ✨ **[Evolution hints](in-the-field.md#evolution-hints)**: stones and held items your party can use
  right now glow in their pocket.
- 🌱 **[Plant from the bag](in-the-field.md#planting-from-the-bag)**: seeds, saplings and berries, without
  leaving the Agriculture pocket.
- 🎯 **[PokeBelt refill](in-the-field.md#pokebelt-refill)**: the satchel restocks the ball you just threw.
- 💾 **[Keep it on death](death.md)**: by default you never lose the bag or what is inside it.
- 🧍 **[On your back](tiers.md#on-your-back)**: each tier is a different 3D bag, dyeable, with a
  cosmetic slot on the Ultra Satchel.
- 🖥️ **[Storage at every PC](pc-storage.md)**: an unlimited item storage, divided by pockets, behind every
  Cobblemon PC.
- ⚙️ **[Tuned in game](commands.md)** with `/satchel config`, no restart needed.
