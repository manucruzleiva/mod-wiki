# Reporting Bugs & Features

Found a bug, or have an idea? You don't need a GitHub account — just post in our **Discord**, and
your message is turned into a tracked issue automatically (and you get [credited](credits.md)!).

## Where to post

| Channel | For |
|---------|-----|
| **#bugs** | something broken or behaving wrong |
| **#features** | an idea, request, or improvement |

Within a few minutes, a bot reads your message, opens an issue on the project's tracker, and adds a
✅ reaction to your message so you know it was logged.

## Writing a good bug report

A little detail goes a long way. Try to include:

- **What happened** vs. **what you expected**.
- **Steps to reproduce** it (what were you doing?).
- Your **versions** — Minecraft, Cobblemon, and Cobblemon Picnic.
- Other mods that might be involved, and a screenshot or log if you have one.

!!! example
    > Placing a Diving table underwater near coral crashes the client. MC 1.21.1, Cobblemon 1.7.3,
    > Picnic 1.24.0. Happens every time; log attached. Only other furniture mod is Handcrafted.

## Writing a good feature request

- **What** you'd like and **why** it would help your playstyle.
- If it's a tweak to something that exists, say what's missing today.

## What happens next

- Your post becomes an issue labelled `bug` or `enhancement` (plus `discord`).
- You're added to the **[Community Credits](credits.md)** page.
- Progress happens on the tracker and ships in a future release — watch the
  [changelog](https://modrinth.com/mod/cobblemon-picnic/changelog).

Thank you for helping make the mod better! 💚
