# Cobblemon Mid-Battle EXP

Earn experience the way the main series pays it: **at every knockout**, not in one lump when the
battle ends. A level gained mid-battle counts in that same battle — new stats, more HP — and a move
it unlocks is yours to take before the next turn.

There is nothing to configure and no command to learn. Install it and fight.

## What can it do?

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

- :material-sword-cross: **[EXP at each knockout](how-it-works.md#experience-and-evs-at-each-knockout)**
  — every Pokémon that fought the one that fainted is paid on the spot, Exp. Share holders too, with
  every Cobblemon multiplier applied. EVs arrive at the same moment.
- :material-arrow-up-bold: **[Levels that count now](how-it-works.md#a-new-level-counts-in-the-same-battle)**
  — new stats and more HP in the battle the level was earned in. Stat boosts you already set up stay.
- :material-cursor-move: **[A move screen, mid-battle](how-it-works.md#the-move-screen)** — when a
  Pokémon that knows four moves unlocks a new one, drag it into the moveset or send it to the move
  pool.
- :material-text-box-outline: **[Everything in the battle log](how-it-works.md#battle-log)** — EXP,
  levels, stats and moves, said to the Pokémon's owner and nobody else.

</div>

## The one rule

**A knockout pays once.** Whatever a knockout paid, the end of the battle does not pay again for the
same opponent. The total is what Cobblemon would have given you; it simply arrives as you earn it.

## Get it

| | |
|---|---|
| **Needs** | Minecraft 1.21.1 on Fabric or NeoForge, [Cobblemon](https://modrinth.com/mod/cobblemon) ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/cobblemon)) 1.7 or 1.8, Architectury API, and Kotlin for your loader |
| **Works with** | any mod that changes how much EXP or how many EVs a battle gives — its numbers are the ones paid |
| **Does not need** | a config file, a command or a permission |

One jar per loader, and the same jar runs on Cobblemon 1.7 and 1.8. Drop the one for your loader into
`mods/`, never both at once.
