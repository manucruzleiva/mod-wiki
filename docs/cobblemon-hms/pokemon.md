# Your Pokémon

Everything in Cobblemon HMs comes from **your Pokémon**. Send one out, open Cobblemon's
**interaction wheel** on it and pick **Field moves**. The second wheel lists everything that Pokémon
can do for you:

- **Partner HMs** it can lend your empty fist — see [Partner HMs](abilities/active.md).
- **Auras** it can give you while it is out and near you — see [Auras](abilities/toggles.md).
- **Mount toggles** that help it when you ride it — below.

A Pokémon can do any of these when **the move is in its movepool** (anything it can learn: by
level, egg, tutor, TM, evolution or form change; it does not need to be one of its four moves), or
when **its species is one listed on these pages** (the species that used to teach the HM).

!!! note "No field moves any more"
    Celebrate is a [partner HM](abilities/active.md#celebrate) now, and Rain Dance, Sunny Day and
    Blizzard are [weather auras](abilities/toggles.md#rain-dance).

## Mount toggles

The toggles that make a ride better belong to the **Pokémon you ride**. Open the same **Field moves**
wheel on it and switch them on there; a toggle that is on shows **green**. They stay on for that
Pokémon — not for you, and not for your other mounts — and help it whenever it is ridden.

A mount toggle only shows up on a Pokémon you can **ride the way it helps**: Tailwind on one you can
fly on, Surf on one you can swim on, Agility on one you can run on. Swift, U-turn and Helping Hand
need a Pokémon you can ride at all. A Pokémon that learns Tailwind but cannot carry you through the
air does not offer it.

The [Jump](abilities/toggles.md#jump) and [Rock Climb](abilities/toggles.md#rock-climb) auras also
carry over to whatever you are riding.
