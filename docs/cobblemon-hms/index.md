# Cobblemon HMs

Your Pokémon lend you their moves. Send one out and it cuts trees, smashes rocks and lights caves
with your bare fist, or gives you auras like Flash, Dive and Rock Climb while it stays near you.

## How it works

1. **Send out a Pokémon** and open Cobblemon's interaction wheel on it.
2. Pick **Field moves**: it lists every HM that Pokémon has **in its movepool**.
3. **Partner HMs** go to your **empty fist**: one at a time, fired with a click.
4. **Auras** run on you while the Pokémon is out and within 24 blocks.
5. No hunger, nothing to learn and nothing to carry: just a cooldown per Pokémon.

!!! abstract "4.0: the companion model"
    You no longer learn HMs. Every HM now comes from a Pokémon of yours that knows the move, the
    Trainer Ditto sells TMs, and the HM Case is a TM bag. [What changed](upgrading-to-4.md).
