# HM Case (TM bag)

Since 4.0 the **HM Case** is a bag for **Cobblemon TMs**. It holds as many as you like, of as many
moves as you like, in one slot.

## Crafting

Shapeless: a **chest**, a **diamond** and one **apricorn of each colour** (red, yellow, green,
blue, black, white and pink).

## Using it

Right-click with the Case in hand to open it.

| Do | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Put TMs away** | Every TM in your inventory goes into the bag |
| **Click** a row | Takes one TM of that move out |
| **Shift-click** a row | Takes up to a full stack (64) out |
| Type in the **search box** | Filters the list by move name |

The Case's tooltip shows how many TMs are inside. You only take out what fits in your inventory;
the rest stays in the bag, so nothing is ever dropped or lost, in creative mode too.

**Only plain TMs go in.** A TM you renamed in an anvil, or one whose move Cobblemon no longer knows,
stays in your inventory when you put TMs away, so its name is not lost. If a move leaves Cobblemon
(a datapack removed, say) while its TMs are already in the bag, they stay there under
"Unknown move" and come back out once the move is back.

The contents are stored on the Case itself, so it keeps them when you put it in a chest or hand it
to a friend.

!!! tip "Where TMs come from"
    The [Trainer Ditto](getting-started.md#the-trainer-ditto) sells three random TMs for relic coins
    every time it visits.
