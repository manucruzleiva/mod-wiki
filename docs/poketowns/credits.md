# Community Credits

PokeTowns stands on other people's work. Thank you! 💚

## Builds

- **Cobblemon Additions** (CC0): the houses, streets, lamp posts, Pokémon Center, Poké Mart, stadium,
  roofed wells, berry planters and the gym Towns' battle pad are its builds, used as stand-ins until
  PokeTowns' own replace them.
- **COBBLEVERSE**: the Kanto gyms and their leaders in gym Towns. PokeTowns reads them from the
  COBBLEVERSE datapack when it is installed and ships none of them.
- **CobbleDollars**: the Poké Mart Clerk is its merchant.
- **Minecraft**: the farms are vanilla's.
