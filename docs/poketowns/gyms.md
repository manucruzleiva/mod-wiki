# Gyms

Three Towns in ten are **gym Towns**: a gym stands behind the Pokémon Center, reached by a path from
the Center's back yard. Otherwise a gym Town grows like any other Town.

Every **City** has the same gym on its square, every time (see [Settlements](settlements.md)).

## With COBBLEVERSE

When the **COBBLEVERSE** datapack is installed, the gym is one of its seven land gyms, with its leader
inside: Brock, Lt. Surge, Erika, Koga, Sabrina, Blaine or Giovanni.

Each gym Town or City picks one of the seven at random. PokeTowns reads the gym from the datapack and ships
none of it, so the building, the leader and the battle are COBBLEVERSE's.

## Without COBBLEVERSE

A battle pad from Cobblemon Additions stands where the gym would be.

## For gym mods

Every gym marks where its Gym Manager, its leader, three disciples, the challenger and both sides'
Pokémon stand, so a gym mod such as PokeGyms can stage the battle. A settlement generated without a
gym mod shows no trace of the marks. The ids are on [For Pack Authors](pack-authors.md#gyms).

## Known limitations

- Two gym Towns or Cities can end up with the same leader; nothing yet hands each leader out only once.
- PokeTowns needs Cobblemon 1.8 or newer. If your copy of the COBBLEVERSE modpack still runs
  Cobblemon 1.7, PokeTowns will not start in it.
