# Gyms

Three Towns in ten are **gym Towns**: a gym stands behind the Pokémon Center, reached by a path from
the Center's back yard. Otherwise a gym Town grows like any other Town.

## With COBBLEVERSE

When the **COBBLEVERSE** datapack is installed, the gym is one of its seven land gyms, with its leader
inside: Brock, Lt. Surge, Erika, Koga, Sabrina, Blaine or Giovanni.

Each gym Town picks one of the seven at random. PokeTowns reads the gym from the datapack and ships
none of it, so the building, the leader and the battle are COBBLEVERSE's.

## Without COBBLEVERSE

A battle pad from Cobblemon Additions stands where the gym would be.

## Known limitations

- Two gym Towns can end up with the same leader; nothing yet hands each leader out only once.
- PokeTowns needs Cobblemon 1.8 or newer. If your copy of the COBBLEVERSE modpack still runs
  Cobblemon 1.7, PokeTowns will not start in it.
