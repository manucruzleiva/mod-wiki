    🧺 Cobblemon Picnic¶

    Cobblemon Picnic is a Cobblemon add-on that turns spawn-hunting into a cozy picnic. Place a Picnic Table, right-click it, and it despawns the nearby wild Pokémon and forces a fresh "maximized" natural respawn around you — a spawn re-roller for hunting shinies or a specific species without roaming the world.

    It's also a social station: sit on the benches for a shiny bonus, care for your Pokémon, store snacks in baskets, and summon trainers to battle.

    Requirements

    Minecraft 1.21.1 · Fabric or NeoForge · Cobblemon 1.7.x. Fabric also needs Fabric API + Fabric Language Kotlin; NeoForge needs Architectury API + Kotlin for Forge. Cloth Config (+ Mod Menu on Fabric) is optional — the in-game settings screen only.

    What can it do?¶

    • Spawn re-rolling — despawn wild Pokémon and force a fresh batch around you, on demand.
    • Four table tiers — Basic, Camping, Glamping, and the underwater-only Diving table.
    • Shiny aura — sit together to raise shiny odds, with a particle ring and a HUD buff.
    • Interactions & items — wash & play with Pokémon, store bread in baskets, bias spawns with snacks.
    • Battle Seeker — a rare item that summons RCT trainers from a Glamping table.
    • Commands & Configuration — full in-game tuning and live statistics.

    Get it¶

    • Download: Modrinth (Fabric & NeoForge) · CurseForge
    • Bugs & ideas: report them in the community Discord — they're forwarded straight to the project's issue tracker.
