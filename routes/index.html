    Routes¶

    Turn any Cobblemon world into a classic-style Pokémon region: physical roads generated during worldgen that connect its cities and structures, named capture zones, and map integration that ties it together. Works with any Cobblemon setup, and connects the gyms of packs like COBBLEVERSE when they are present.

    MC 1.21.1 Cobblemon 1.7.3 Fabric NeoForge

    Get started Commands

    Highlights¶

    • 🗺️ Dynamic routes between cities and structures, fully part of worldgen — roads seek flat ground, stay off the coast, tunnel mineshaft-style through mountains, and get names like Route 3: Steep Forest Pass.
    • 🎮 Hardcore Nuzlocke and ❤️‍🔥 Soul Link: per-zone encounter lock, permadeath and linked co-op runs. These belong to the Nuzlocke & Soul Link add-on — which ships inside this jar for now, so you get them either way. The zones they lock onto are the routes, areas and towns generated here.
    • 🧭 Map integration (optional): Waystones city nodes plus Xaero's waypoints, route trails and chunk painting.
    • 🧗 Escape Rope: a craftable one-tap trip back to the nearest known town — works with or without Waystones.
    • 🧩 World-creation tabs: dedicated NUZLOCKE and ROUTES tabs on the create-world screen — every rule set up-front, saved per world.

    Get it¶

    Download for Fabric or NeoForge from Modrinth or CurseForge, then see Getting Started. Questions or ideas? Join the Discord.
