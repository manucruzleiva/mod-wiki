    🧗 Items¶

    Cobblemon Routes adds a small toolkit for life on the road. Right now that's the Escape Rope — a one-tap ticket back to town.

    🧗 Escape Rope¶

    Use it and you're whisked to the nearest known town on the route network — and because every activated waystone registers as a town, it works whether or not you run the Waystones mod.

    • Where it sends you — the closest discovered city / town, landing on a safe, dry column (the same spot-finder that places a new world's spawn).
    • Restrictions — Overworld only, never mid-battle, with a short 10-second cooldown.
    • Cost — consumes one rope per use (free in creative). One craft makes two.

    Tip

    No known town yet? The rope politely refuses and tells you so — go discover a village or activate a waystone first.

    🪢 Crafting¶

    A shapeless recipe — drop the ingredients anywhere in the crafting grid:

    ➜
    2
    Ingredient Amount
    String 2
    Ender Pearl 1
    → Escape Rope 2

    Note

    Because the recipe is shapeless, the arrangement in the 3×3 grid doesn't matter — just have the two String and one Ender Pearl in the table together.
