    Partner HMs¶

    Partner HMs are the ones your empty fist fires. One of your sent-out Pokémon lends you one: open Cobblemon's interaction wheel on it, pick Field moves, then "Lend me …". While that Pokémon is out and within 24 blocks, a click with an empty hand fires the HM. One Pokémon lends one HM at a time: picking another replaces it.

    The only cost is a cooldown, kept per Pokémon: two Pokémon that both know Cut each have their own.

    1. Do Click with an empty hand
    2. Check A Pokémon of yours, out and near you, is lending the HM
    3. Check Your experience level is high enough (if the server sets one)
    4. Check That Pokémon's cooldown has run out
    5. Result The HM fires and that Pokémon's cooldown starts

    If any check fails, nothing happens.

    Who can lend it¶

    A Pokémon can lend an HM when the move is in its movepool (equipped or not) — including a move merged into it, so a Pokémon with Dig can lend Rock Smash — or when its species is one of these:

    Disc HM Pokémon (any of)
    Camouflage Zorua, Kecleon, Ditto
    Charge Elekid, Electabuzz, Electivire
    Cut Scyther, Scizor, Kartana, Kleavor, Vikavolt
    Leafage Bulbasaur, Chikorita, Treecko, Snivy, Drilbur, Excadrill
    Rock Smash Hitmonchan, Machop, Geodude, Diglett, Dugtrio, Trapinch
    Stockpile Gulpin, Swalot, Squirtle, Totodile, Mudkip, Camerupt, Magcargo, Heatran
    Strength Machoke, Machamp, Conkeldurr
    Tail Whip Rattata, Sentret, Buneary
    Teleport Abra, Kadabra, Alakazam, Ralts
    Growth Sunkern, Sunflora, Petilil
    Minimize Ditto, Chansey, Barboach
    Outrage Dragonite, Salamence, Haxorus
    Calm Mind Alakazam, Espeon, Gardevoir
    Celebrate Jigglypuff, Ludicolo, Alcremie

    Ability details¶

    Camouflage¶

    Cooldown: 15 min · Power: 18000t (15 minutes)

    Become the living creature you're looking at. Your model is replaced by theirs — species, form, variant, gear and its nametag — for everyone who can see you.

    • Aim at a creature in clear view → you look exactly like it for 15 minutes.
    • Fire it again while you are wearing one → the costume comes off and the HM is ready immediately, so changing disguise is two presses.
    • A block, a wall between you and it, or empty air → nothing happens. You can only copy something you can actually see.
    • Other players can't be copied.

    The disguise is a costume, not a creature: nothing extra exists in the world, so there is nothing to hit, nothing in your way, and nothing that can be killed off you.

    Charge¶

    Cooldown: 3s · Power: ticks between sparks (10)

    Turns the block you aim at into a redstone power source — enough to throw a door, a piston or a lamp from across a room. The charge is permanent. It is saved with the world, so it stays when you log off and when the server restarts, and charging another block does not take it back: charge as many as you like. Aim Charge at a charged block again to discharge it. Breaking the block ends its charge too.

    The block is never replaced — it stays exactly what it is and simply answers as a power source — so you can charge a chest, a furnace, a statue or a wall without losing anything. While it is live it throws off electric sparks, so a charged block is one you can find again.

    Cut¶

    Power: max 128 blocks

    Aim at a log and the whole tree comes down — every connected log, with the leaves and vines tangled in it. Aim at a leaf or a vine and just that tangle goes.

    It also cuts plants: grass, ferns, crops, ground cover. Those come away as if you had used shears, so the drops are the careful ones — the grass itself rather than a couple of seeds — and that holds for plants added by a datapack too, because it is the game's own loot tables answering.

    With nothing to cut in front of you, it clears the ground around you (this is what X-Scissor used to do), in two passes of decreasing politeness:

    1. Weeds first — grass, ferns, dead bushes. If there are any, that is all it touches.
    2. Then what somebody planted — flowers and crops, and only once there is not a single weed left in range (radius 5). Casting by accident in your own wheat field costs you the weeds, not the field.

    It never strips grass down to dirt: as a fallback, that would go off every time you missed a tree.

    Leafage¶

    Cooldown: 2s · Power: radius 5

    Instantly grows every crop around you to full maturity — handy for fast harvests — and does the same for saplings and berry trees.

    The bare ground answers too, and it answers in flowers: a burst leaves a meadow rather than a lawn, with the odd blade of grass among it. The blooms are ones that belong to the biome you are standing in, so a burst in a plains looks nothing like one in a flower forest — and a biome with no flowers of its own gets grass rather than a dandelion that does not belong there.

    It only ever plants on bare ground. A spot that already has something growing on it is left exactly as it stands, so casting twice in the same meadow thickens it instead of disturbing it, and tall flowers are planted whole rather than as a half that topples.

    Crouch while you use it to till instead (this is what Rototiller used to do): the dirt-type block you aim at — Dirt, Grass Block, Coarse Dirt, Dirt Path — turns into Farmland.

    Rock Smash¶

    Power: max 32 ores

    Instantly breaks the block you aim at. If it's an ore, vein-mines all connected ore of that type; otherwise breaks the single pickaxe-minable block (Netherite Pickaxe drops).

    It digs too (this is what Dig used to do): dirt, sand, gravel and anything else a shovel takes come out in one hit, free, with a Netherite Shovel's drops.

    Stockpile¶

    Cooldown: 1s · Power: lava self-damage 7 (3.5 hearts)

    Stockpile is a mouthful you keep. It took over Water Gun and Eruption.

    • Aim at water or lava (source or flowing) and you drink it. The pool is drained, and that fluid is now what you carry. Water is free; lava is spicy and burns going down.
    • Aim anywhere else and you spit it back out on the face you are aiming at — the same square a bucket would fill. You can spit as often as you like: what you carry only changes when you drink the other fluid.
    • Spitting water at a sponge soaks it.

    It passes the same permission check a bucket does, so claims, spawn protection and the world border all hold. Lava is never placed at your own feet, and water boils away in the Nether, as it would from a bucket.

    Strength¶

    Pushes the aimed block one block in whichever of the six directions you're looking — up and down included. Containers travel with their contents, and a double chest moves as one unit: both halves go together, or the push fails if either has nowhere to go. Won't move fluids.

    Tail Whip¶

    Cooldown: 2s

    Sweeps a suspicious sand or gravel block clean in a single motion instead of a dozen slow ones, and finds exactly what a brush would have found. Rooted dirt counts too: it is not brushable in vanilla, but it is the same gesture on the same kind of thing, so it gives up its hanging roots.

    Teleport¶

    Cooldown: 30s · Power: max 30 blocks

    Blinks you to the block you're looking at (≤ 30 blocks), finding the nearest safe landing. Plays the Enderman teleport sound.

    It is the strongest way to travel the mod has, so it is priced like it: dearer than anything else you fire, and slow to come back.

    Growth¶

    Cooldown: 1 min · Power: how long you stay big (5 minutes)

    Aim at one of your Pokémon and it grows one size, for good. Aim at anything else, or at nothing, and it is you who grows, to twice your size, for a while.

    Minimize¶

    Cooldown: 1 min · Power: how long you stay small (5 minutes)

    Growth's opposite. Aim at a Pokémon and it shrinks one size, for good. Aim anywhere else and you go half-size for a while.

    Outrage¶

    Cooldown: 5 min

    Aim at an XL Pokémon to turn it into an Alpha. Anything smaller, it refuses.

    Calm Mind¶

    Cooldown: 2 min

    Aim at an Alpha to settle it back into the ordinary Pokémon it was. The way back out of Outrage.

    Celebrate¶

    Cooldown: 10 min · Power: how many rockets (8)

    Punch with an empty hand and a firework show goes up over your head, for no reason at all. The rockets are made for you, not taken from your inventory, and burst well above head height, so they hurt nobody.
